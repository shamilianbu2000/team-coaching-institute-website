## Not found / client confirmation required
- Official logo and brand colours (website uses a proposed navy/yellow identity until a logo is supplied).
- Authentic photographs the client approves for publication (listing photos were not copied: licence unknown).
- Exact classes and standards taught (Classes 1–7 are NOT assumed; only 8–12 and NEET appear in public reviews), subjects, batch timings, fees, eligibility, syllabus/board.
- Faculty names, qualifications, experience, photos.
- Genuine results, achievements, awards, student counts, testimonials with permission.
- Whether a demo class is offered (the website has no demo-class CTA until confirmed).
- WhatsApp number (the verified phone number is used as the default; confirm it is WhatsApp-enabled).
- Email address, opening time, founder/management details, year established, vision/mission.
- Instagram, YouTube, Facebook or other social profiles (none found).
- Custom domain, Google Analytics 4 measurement ID, Formspree endpoint and receiving email.

## Content rules used on the website
1. Only the "verified / consistent" table items are stated as fact.
2. Everything else is marked "Client confirmation required".
3. No review text is copied; only themes and a link to the live Google reviews are shown.
4. No ratings are shown except the Google Maps figure with source attribution.
